# Memory, traces, and the out-of-band pass

## Who has a memory

**Two, separate.**

| Who | Remembers |
|---|---|
| **the judge** | **what has already slipped past it** |
| **the builder** | **what it has already tried** |

⚠️ **They don't merge.** Merging them would put back together the two things this method keeps apart on
purpose, for the same reason the judge doesn't see the reasoning.

⚠️ **No schema.** Don't prescribe memory types: **imposing them makes results worse**, and the source
says so. You write **who remembers what**, not with which fields.

⚠️ **And a memory that grows is paid for every session**: it falls under the ceiling, it isn't exempt.

## The defect of memory written while working

**What gets written in-band stays wrong there until somebody rereads it cold.** Measured by the source:
with raw memory, **five replicates out of five** fall into the same pit; with an out-of-band pass the
error corrects.

⚠️ And on this method the defect showed up **six times in two days**: accusations born from a cut
sentence, over-generous acquittals, corrections that widened another citation, a miscount, and **seven
errors introduced by the remedy**. The cure attempted until then was **more in-band discipline**; the
source says that isn't it.

## What the out-of-band pass may do

⚠️ **It MARKS a superseded line with the reopened source. It does NOT rewrite it.** Rewriting belongs
to whoever reopens the question.

**The reason**: a rewrite made with nobody watching is **indistinguishable from a wrong rewrite**:
it's the hallucinated ✅ applied to the archive. Marking already removes the harm (nobody trusts that
line any more) and costs a human **only when it's actually needed**.

⚠️ **What stays open, and gets said**: the criterion for **what's worth saving** (distillation) isn't
decided. What was decided is **who corrects**, not **what goes in**. Until that criterion exists, a
folder where everyone writes whatever they want **buys the very defect this pass exists to cure**.

## Traces

| Rule | In one line |
|---|---|
| **Always a file** | **even when clean**: *absent* must not be able to mean both *clean* and *never ran* |
| **Delivery is a file** | not a message. A message can fail to arrive without **either side** knowing |
| **Who rereads them** | ⚠️ **not decided, and declared**: the traces exist and no step consumes them. The primary debugging move is *reading what the agent actually did and finding where its judgment diverges from yours*, but without somebody reopening them, they're an archive |
| **Who fixes the prompt** | ⚠️ **the source prescribes an agent that combs the traces and updates the instructions itself**, i.e. **closing the loop on the harness too**. Today traces get opened by hand and only to recover lost deliveries: it's the step missing for the method to correct **itself** rather than being corrected each round by a person |
| **What they don't cover** | the **hallucinated ✅**: the contract closes *"report missing"*, not *"report wrong"* |

## Compaction

⚠️ **The builder inherits the context**: a clean context is a property of the **judge**, and imposing
it on the builder costs re-explaining everything and buys nothing.

**The builder's cure is a different one, and it's about size: it's called the DECLARED STOP POINT**,
⚠️ **and the threshold is declared by whoever builds, in the step 0
announcement, before starting.** ⚠️ **And the announcement has a box for it**, otherwise the rule has a
moment and no form to write it on: a number picked once you're already inside is a surrender, not a
threshold. Above that declared threshold **you write
the state to a document, restart from there, and then throw away the context that produced it**. The
source says it whole: the plan is worth a few thousand tokens, the research that produced it a hundred
times that, and **that part gets thrown away**.

⚠️ **An append-only log is not the same as compacting**: both ways of shrinking context **destroy**, a
log doesn't. Where a log is possible, keep it.
